From truth table to simpler logic.

Choose your cells

For minterms, mark the combinations where the output is 1. For maxterms, mark the combinations where the output is 0.

02

Find the pattern

K-maps arrange combinations in Gray-code order. Adjacent 1s or 0s can be grouped in powers of two, including across edges.

03

Read the result

The simulator calculates a minimized SOP or POS expression and displays the corresponding truth table.
